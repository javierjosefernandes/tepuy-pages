About

Hatilú Studio is Javier Fernandes, an independent Apple developer in the Netherlands. There is nobody else: I design, build and support every app myself.

I work in health tech, where protecting people’s data is the job, and I hold my own apps to the same standard. They don’t ask you for an account, and what you put in them stays yours.

I was born and grew up in Venezuela. The name joins El Hatillo, the town where I grew up, with the first letters of my eldest daughter’s name, Lucia.
