In a paragraph

Hatilú Studio is the one-person app studio of Javier Fernandes, an Apple developer based in the Netherlands who was born and grew up in Venezuela. He works in health tech and builds his own apps on the same principle: no ads, no account to create, and the user’s data stays the user’s. The studio’s first app is Tepuy, a workout tracker whose logging is free with no limits.

The name

Hatilú joins El Hatillo, the town in Venezuela where Javier grew up, with the first letters of his eldest daughter’s name, Lucia. It is written with an accent on the u.
