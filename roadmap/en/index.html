Tepuy

Roadmap — Hatilú Studio · reflects app version 1.16.0

Built for athletes who take their training seriously. Here’s what Tepuy can do for you today — and what’s coming next.

What You Can Do Today

Everything you need to design, track, and understand your training — free and without limits.

Design Your Training

Create custom exercises for any discipline. Build workout templates that match exactly how you train — your movements, your structure, your way.

Track Every Session

Log workouts from a template or go freestyle. Record sets, reps, weight, and more. Forgot to log a session? Add it after the fact.

Plan Your Week

Schedule sessions on the calendar — at a specific time, or any time of day. Get reminders so you don’t miss one, and mirror everything to Apple Calendar.

Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

Your active workout stays on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — check your timer and current set at a glance.

Home Screen Widgets

Glance at your next workout, your goals, and your weekly insights right from your Home Screen.

Apple Health

Your workouts show up in Apple Health — duration, distance, and calories included. Calorie estimates are smart, too: they factor in your activity, your pace, and your body weight.

See Your Progress

Charts that show how you’re improving over time. Personal records tracked automatically. Progression insights for every exercise and workout.

Body Measurements

Log your weight, body fat, and measurements like waist, chest, and arms — and watch them change over time with charts. They sync to Apple Health, too.

Goals & Streaks

Set targets, track consistency, and build momentum with streaks that keep you accountable. A badge reminds you of pending workouts.

Your Dashboard

Today’s snapshot, quick actions, recent activity, and your latest PRs — all in one place.

Works Everywhere

Optimized for iPhone and iPad. Syncs seamlessly across your devices with iCloud. Available in 8 languages.

Apple Watch

Start today’s planned workout right from your wrist. Move through your session, record results and comments, manage rest and work timers, and finish with Apple Health recording — all while your iPhone keeps everything in sync.

Share Your Workouts

Export your workouts and exercises as self-contained Tepuy files. Anyone you send them to can preview before importing and decide how to handle anything already in their library.

Share Your Sessions

Turn a finished session into a polished Card for a quick visual recap, or a detailed PDF Report with planned and completed results, feeling, and comments — ready to share anywhere.

Premium Companions

Workout tracking is — and will always be — completely free. That’s the foundation of Tepuy, and we’re not putting it behind a paywall. Premium adds optional companion tools that go beyond training logs: deeper insights, personalized guidance, and wellness tracking. They’re here to complement your training, never to limit it.

AI Coaching

Your personal coach, available anytime. Get tailored advice on workouts, nutrition, cooking, and recovery — all informed by your actual training data.

Nutrition & Supplements

Log meals, macros, supplements, and hydration. Set reminders and track adherence.

Recovery

Monitor sleep quality, readiness, fatigue, and keep a pain and injury log.

Advanced Insights

Post-workout analytics, long-term trends, cross-domain correlations, and smart recommendations to take your training further.

Further Out

New Disciplines

Hyrox and HIIT / circuit training — tracked and understood just like every other discipline.
