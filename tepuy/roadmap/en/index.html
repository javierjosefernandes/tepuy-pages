Tepuy

Roadmap — Hatilú Studio · reflects app version 1.23.0

Built for athletes who take their training seriously. Here’s what Tepuy can do for you today — and what’s coming next.

What You Can Do Today

Everything you need to design, track, and understand your training — free, with no limits.

Design Your Training

Create custom exercises for any discipline. Build workout templates that match exactly how you train — your movements, your structure, your way.

Create Exercises with AI

Copy a Tepuy prompt into your preferred AI app, answer its guided questions, then preview and import up to 50 generated exercises from a .tepuy file.

Create Routines with AI

Choose up to 10 exercises — including combined exercises — then copy Tepuy’s guided prompt into your preferred AI app. It builds one goal-driven routine for you to review and import.

Track Every Session

Log workouts from a template or go freestyle. Record sets, reps, weight, and more. Forgot to log a session? Add it after the fact.

Hyrox Racing

Log a Hyrox race — an official event or a simulation in the gym — after the fact, or race it live from a Hyrox routine. Record your total time, the split for every run and station, your division, and your places. Each race opens a results-style screen with your transitions, pacing, and records, compared with your previous race. Tepuy keeps your fastest race for each course and division, and your fastest time for every run and station. Full Hyrox and Half Hyrox routines come in the built-in library, and races get their own share Card.

Plan Your Week

Schedule sessions on the calendar — at a specific time, or any time of day. Get reminders so you don’t miss one, and mirror everything to Apple Calendar.

Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

Your active workout stays on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — check your timer and current set at a glance.

Home Screen Widgets

Glance at your next workout, your goals, and your weekly insights right from your Home Screen.

Apple Health

Your workouts show up in Apple Health — duration, distance, and calories included. Calorie estimates are smart, too: they factor in your activity, your pace, and your body weight. Each distance lands in its own type — walking and running, rowing, or cycling — and functional strength training and HIIT workouts are recorded as exactly that.

See Your Progress

Charts that show how you’re improving over time. Personal records tracked automatically. Progression insights for every exercise and workout. Insights sums up your week in one line, opens on this week with a trend behind every metric, and adds your race times and fastest times once you race.

Body Measurements

Log your weight, body fat, and measurements like waist, chest, and arms — and watch them change over time with charts. They sync to Apple Health, too.

Goals & Streaks

Set targets, track consistency, and build momentum with streaks that keep you accountable. A badge reminds you of pending workouts. Race time goals set a target time for a Hyrox course and division, with an optional deadline.

Your Dashboard

Today’s snapshot, quick actions, recent activity, and your latest PRs — all in one place.

Works Everywhere

Optimized for iPhone and iPad. Syncs seamlessly across your devices with iCloud. Available in 8 languages — right down to the built-in workout library, fully translated. Metric or imperial: Tepuy starts with your region’s units and uses them for every value you see or enter.

Apple Watch

Start today’s planned workout right from your wrist — or straight from the Next Up watch face widget, which shows today’s plan at a glance. Your session follows the same flow as on iPhone: rest countdown, current set, and work timer right where you expect them. Record results and comments, reset a set if you need to, and finish with Apple Health recording — all while your iPhone keeps everything in sync. If your iPhone isn’t around, reopen Tepuy on your Watch to pick up your active workout.

Share Your Workouts

Export your workouts and exercises as self-contained Tepuy files. Anyone you send them to can preview before importing and decide how to handle anything already in their library.

Share Your Sessions

Turn a finished session into a polished Card for a quick visual recap, or a detailed PDF Report with planned and completed results, feeling, and comments — ready to share anywhere.

Companions

Logging is free, intelligence is premium. That holds for everything Tepuy adds: what you log, and its charts, stay free and unlimited. Premium is what Tepuy works out for you from it — optional, and never at the expense of what you already have.

Nutrition & Supplements

Log meals, macros, supplements, and hydration, with reminders. A food database for macros is premium, since every lookup has a cost.

Recovery

Log sleep, readiness, and fatigue, and keep a pain and injury log.

Premium

AI Coaching

Your personal coach, available anytime. Advice on your training, help deciding what to train, and plans built for you — all informed by what you’ve logged, nutrition and recovery included.

Advanced Insights

Correlations between your body, intakes, recovery, and performance, and smart recommendations to take your training further.

Further Out

Strava

Post your finished sessions to Strava, where your training partners already are.

New Disciplines

Structured conditioning — HIIT, circuits, and CrossFit-style WODs — and running as a first-class discipline, tracked and understood just like every other one.
